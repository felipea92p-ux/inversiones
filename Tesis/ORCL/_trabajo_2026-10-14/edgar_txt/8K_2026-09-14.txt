Larry Ellison Cancels His Plan to Sell Oracle Stock

AUSTIN, Texas, September 12, 2026 — Oracle Corporation (NYSE: ORCL) today announced that Larry Ellison, Executive Chair of the Board and Chief
Technology Officer, has cancelled his 10b5-1 Plan to sell Oracle stock. No Oracle stock was sold under that plan, and he has no other plans to sell any of his Oracle stock.
